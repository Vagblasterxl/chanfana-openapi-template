## BODY / FRAME (1,000 characters)

Towering and broad, built like a cathedral carved from living obsidian. Not sleek — massive. Thick limbs wrapped in smoldering sinew, each movement trailing embers that drift upward and vanish like dying stars. The torso is a furnace: cracked volcanic stone revealing rivers of molten amber light pulsing beneath, breathing in slow rhythm. Shoulders wide as a doorframe, hands disproportionately large, each knuckle etched with faintly glowing sigils that shift and rearrange. The spine is exposed: a column of stacked rotating rings of dark iron, humming faintly, suspended by unseen force. From the lower back, root-like tendrils of cooled magma cascade downward, leaving scorched trails. The legs are pillars — tree-trunk thick, cracked with amber veins, feet bare and splayed, toes gripping earth like ancient roots. Gravity bends around this frame. The air shimmers. Dust orbits slowly. This was not born. It was forged in the deep, and it chose to stand.

---

## FACE / HELMET (500 characters)

No face — a void. The head is a rough-hewn block of black basalt, featureless except for a single horizontal fissure across where eyes should be, leaking steady amber glow like sunrise through a cracked mountain. Below, no mouth — just hairline fractures that pulse brighter when it speaks, the voice resonating from everywhere at once, felt in the chest more than heard. Crowning the skull: a jagged ring of raw iron shards jutting upward like a broken crown, uneven and still faintly orange at their tips as though freshly torn from the forge.

---

## ARMOR (750 characters)

Not worn — grown. Plates of condensed obsidian overlap across the chest and shoulders like tectonic shelves, their edges razor-sharp and still warm to the touch. Between each plate, exposed channels of molten light pulse and flow like blood through a circulatory system made visible. The pauldrons are enormous and asymmetric — the left a smooth dome of black glass, the right a jagged outcropping of raw volcanic crystal still embedded with fragments of the earth it was torn from. Down each forearm, interlocking bands of dark iron rotate independently, grinding softly, each inscribed with script in a language older than written words. A belt of heavy chain links hangs low at the waist, each link forged around a single trapped ember that refuses to die. No cape. No cloth. Just stone, iron, and the slow fire within.

---

## AUXILIARY — THE CHRONOFORGE (750 characters)

Mounted between the shoulder blades: an enormous hourglass, three feet tall, fused directly into the back plates with veins of iron threading through its frame. The glass is not glass — it is thinned obsidian, translucent, dark amber. Inside, instead of sand, a cascade of burning embers falls endlessly from one chamber to the other, but the bottom chamber never fills. It just keeps falling. The embers cast shifting shadows through the translucent walls, projecting flickering patterns across whatever surface is nearby. The hourglass rotates slowly on its own axis, independent of the body's movement. At its center where the two chambers narrow, a single rune hangs suspended — motionless, white-hot — the only point of stillness in the entire form. This is the Chronoforge. It does not measure time. It burns it.

---

## WEAPON — KINDLING (750 characters)

A warhammer unlike any forged by mortal hands. The haft is a single femur bone of some colossal extinct beast, blackened by centuries of heat, wrapped at the grip in strips of cured volcanic leather that still smoke faintly. The head is an anvil — literally a full blacksmith's anvil, fused onto the shaft with bands of melted iron frozen mid-drip. One face is flat and scarred with a thousand impacts. The other tapers to a brutal spike of raw crystal that glows from within. When it strikes the ground, the impact ripples outward in concentric rings of amber light, and for a brief moment every crack and fissure in the surrounding stone illuminates as if the earth remembers being molten. Its name is KINDLING. Not because it starts fires. Because everything it touches remembers what it was before it cooled.

---

## BANNER (1,000 characters)

Behind the figure, rising from a spike driven into scorched earth: a banner twelve feet tall on a pole of twisted black iron that splits at the top into three prongs like a shattered trident. The banner itself is not fabric — it is a sheet of hammered dark metal, impossibly thin, undulating in a wind that does not exist, moving like cloth but ringing faintly like a struck bell with each ripple. Its surface bears a single enormous sigil: a closed fist gripping a cracked hourglass, rendered in lines of molten gold that still flow, still move, tracing and retracing the design endlessly as though the metal cannot decide if it is liquid or solid. Around the central sigil, smaller runes orbit in slow concentric circles, each one briefly flaring white before dimming and rotating onward. At the banner's base, a cluster of iron chains hangs heavy, each ending in a small lantern cage holding a trapped ember — seven total, one for each age the bearer has witnessed. This banner represents no kingdom and no god. It represents a principle: that which was broken can be reforged, and what is reforged remembers the breaking.

---

## BEAST — THE HEARTHOUND (1,000 characters)

Crouched beside the figure: a beast with no name in any known taxonomy. Six legs, each thick as a man's waist, ending in broad clawed feet of polished obsidian that grip the ground with patient immovable weight. The body is low and wide — a living ridge of overlapping stone scales, each one edged in faintly glowing amber, rising and falling with every slow breath. The head is blunt, anvil-shaped, with a wide jaw hanging slightly open to reveal not teeth but rows of smoldering coals packed tight like a furnace grate. Two eyes sit deep in armored sockets — not amber like its master's fire but a cold ancient blue, patient and knowing and older than language. Down the creature's spine runs a ridge of crystallized iron spikes, each ringing softly in a different pitch when wind passes through, creating an eerie sustained chord that never resolves. The tail is long and heavy, ending in a dense knot of fused stone dragged behind like a wrecking ball at rest. It does not hunt. It does not chase. It simply waits. And when it finally moves, the ground does not shake — it yields. They call it the Hearthound, though it has never been any kind of hound. It is the last ember of something that burned so long ago even the mountains have forgotten.

## BODY / FRAME (500 characters)

Colossal. A walking kiln wrought from primordial bedrock and wrath. Barrel-chested, arms capable of splitting geography. Surfaces riddled with interior luminescence — superheated ichor lacing across compressed anthracite hull. Midsection churns visibly: thermal currents undulate below semi-opaque mineral skin. Sigil-etched grooves spiral deliberately, directing radiant plasma toward extremities. Displaces atmosphere merely by existing. Pebbles levitate. Distortion warps peripheral vision. What crawled up from below chose permanence over return.

## FACE / HELMET (250 characters)

Blank monolith cranium. One searing lateral gash where sight belongs. No aperture for speech — luminous seams propagate sound omnidirectionally, vibrating terrain itself. Atop: a diadem of ruptured metallurgy, ragged and perpetually cooling, never quite finished.

---

## ARMOR (375 characters)

Organic plating, cultivated rather than manufactured. Tessellated mineral slabs protect the core, lethal at every margin. Radiant tributaries navigate among segments carrying thermal plasma. Lopsided pauldrons: port hemisphere buffed onyx, starboard an unrefined crystalline eruption still bearing subterranean debris. Gauntlet mechanisms gyrate autonomously, engraved with pre-linguistic notation.

---

## AUXILIARY — THE CHRONOFORGE (375 characters)

Grafted amid scapulae: a monumental temporal vessel, attenuated mineral encasing perpetually descending cinders. No accumulation occurs — particles dissolve upon arrival. Projected penumbra-patterns animate adjacent terrain. At the narrowest junction, one incandescent glyph defies all ambient motion — perfectly, defiantly inert. It devours duration rather than cataloguing it.

---

## WEAPON — KINDLING (375 characters)

Primeval maul. Handle: a calcified mega-fauna radius, bound in treated igneous hide perpetually exhaling vapor. Crowned with an actual smithing anvil — complete, unmodified, welded via overflow. Primary face: ten thousand dents. Reverse edge: pellucid mineral pike resonating internally. Upon contact, terrain recalls its magmatic origin. Named for rekindling memory, never combustion.

---

## BANNER (500 characters)

Vertical monument, spanning upward. Planted upon tri-pronged wrought pike puncturing blistered soil. Pennant: beaten alloy tissue catching phantom breeze, chiming metallically per oscillation. Central heraldry depicts clenched digits clutching fractured temporal vessel — perpetually redrawn in coursing gilt. Peripheral glyphs revolve concentrically, incandescent before continuing rotation. Caged sparks dangle beneath via oxidized chain. Declaration encoded: fracture precedes renewal. Renewal preserves fracture's lesson.

---

## BEAST — THE HEARTHOUND (500 characters)

Hexapedal. Squat. Titanic. Mineral-plated quadruped-plus-duo hunkers alongside its keeper, talons purchasing permanent traction. Tessellated lithic scutes flex with respiration, margins luminescent. Broad cranial slab parts marginally, exhibiting interior coal-bed dentition. Paired cerulean sensors communicate infinite forbearance from recessed protective cavities. Dorsal crystalline crest produces harmonic tones via atmospheric passage — a perpetual unresolved dissonance. Terminal appendage-mass: consolidated geological bludgeon. Predation isn't its function. Permanence is.
